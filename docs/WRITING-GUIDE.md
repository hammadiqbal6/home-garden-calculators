# YardTally writing guide (human-quality)

Every guide and calculator body on the site must read as written by an experienced DIY homeowner or landscaper — not flagged as AI-generated by common detectors (GPTZero, ZeroGPT, Originality-style classifiers).

**Detector target:** AI probability **&lt; 30%** on the local detector in `scripts/audit/ai-detect.mjs` (or its Python backend). Score table: [`docs/ai-detect-scores.md`](ai-detect-scores.md).

## Voice

- Write as a practitioner: DIY homeowner / landscaper / small-job estimator. First-hand, practical tone.
- Use specific situations (“on a 10×12 shed slab…”) and real worked numbers that match YardTally calculator outputs and Vitest math.
- Vary sentence length and structure a lot (**burstiness**): mix short punchy sentences with longer ones. Use contractions (`you'll`, `don't`, `it's`).
- Include specific, verifiable details and mild opinions when they help (“I'd round up to the next quarter yard; short loads cost more than the extra concrete”).
- **Never invent** statistics, quotes, named customer stories, or credentials.

## Banned AI tells

Do not use:

- “delve”, “in today's fast-paced world”, “it's important to note”, “navigate”, “landscape” (as a verb for “situation”), “robust”, “seamless”, “leverage”, “unlock”, “elevate”, “comprehensive guide”, “in conclusion”
- “whether you're a … or a …”, “game-changer”, “dive into”
- Em-dash chains, triads of adjectives, every section ending with a summary sentence
- Uniform bullet lists of 3–5 bolded items repeated across pages

Also avoid the filler called out in [`.cursor/rules/content-quality.mdc`](../.cursor/rules/content-quality.mdc): “look no further”, “in today's world”, emoji-heavy intros.

## Structure (keep SEO)

- **Answer-first** paragraph **40–60 words** (sourced verdict first when one exists).
- Question-phrased H2s from real search / PAA questions.
- Worked example with real numbers matching `src/lib/calculators` / Vitest.
- FAQ 3–5 items; FAQ schema only for Q&A that is visible on the page.
- Guides: body ≥800 words in `guideBodies.ts`; Sources list; Reviewed-by byline.
- Tools: calculator above the fold; link ≥1 topical guide; TrustBlock sources.
- Keep URLs, primary keywords, internal links, schema wiring, and sourced bag yields / densities intact.

## Article shape

- Don't open every section the same way; avoid symmetrical structure across articles.
- Prefer prose over stacks of identical bullet blocks. Lists are fine when they are purposeful (bag charts, shopping takeoffs).
- Keep tables and source links; rewrite the surrounding sentences for human rhythm.

## Verification

1. Rewrite copy to the rules above.
2. Run `node scripts/audit/ai-detect.mjs` (installs / uses a local Hugging Face detector when available).
3. Target **&lt; 30% AI** per guide/tool body. Rewrite and re-test until it passes (or document best-effort + model limits in `docs/ai-detect-scores.md`).
4. Put the per-article score table in the PR description and keep `docs/ai-detect-scores.md` in sync.
